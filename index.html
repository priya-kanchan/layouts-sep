OVERVIEW

THE
OVERVIEW

Godrej Verano Sector 63A, Gurgaon is an exclusive residential development designed for those who aspire to a refined and luxurious lifestyle. Located in the prestigious Sector 63A of Gurgaon, this mid-rise gated community offers an unparalleled living experience with 2, 3, and 4 BHK apartments. Spanning across 10 acres of lush greenery, this project harmoniously blends modern architecture with natural surroundings.

Featuring a total of 650 premium apartments, Godrej Verano offers a limited collection of low-rise residences that provide privacy, tranquility, and exclusivity. With 2 acres dedicated to beautifully landscaped parks and green spaces, residents can experience a serene, resort-style living environment. Situated along the coveted Golf Course Extension Road, the project enjoys seamless connectivity to key city hubs.
